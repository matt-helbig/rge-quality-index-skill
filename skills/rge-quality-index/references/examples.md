## Polished but generic promotion

**Evidence:** Complete HTML and renders show clear typography, working hierarchy, and appropriate alternatives. The offer and execution are interchangeable with the category. No specific teachable choice stands out.

**Judgment:** Strong craft can coexist with an Interchangeable distinctiveness tier. Decline for lack of editorial distinction; report no critical issues observed within the reviewed evidence. Do not invent usability fixes or demand decoration to justify the decline.

## Restrained password reset

**Evidence:** Clear sender identity, an understandable request, one prominent reset action, and explanatory context if the recipient did not request it. The source and supplied renders support accessible execution. No journey data or post-click test is supplied.

**Judgment:** Evaluate comprehension and task completion, not persuasion or upsell. System fonts and “Reset password” are appropriate. Keep untested link behavior explicit. A conventional but sound implementation may be declined for the gallery; an unusually instructive service interaction may earn a reason to feature. Do not award or deny gallery merit merely because it is transactional.

## One exceptional idea, otherwise competent

## Beautiful but inaccessible

**Evidence:** The HTML confirms that essential message and action depend entirely on images; only a legal footer is live. The layout is visually memorable. Alternative text is present.

**Judgment:** Preserve the specific visual idea in feedback. Apply the RGE all-image ceiling (P2 ≤2.9 and final ≤3.4), decline the current execution, and explain the needed live-text treatment. Do not claim that alt text provides no screen-reader path, and do not waive the editorial ceiling because the images are attractive.
